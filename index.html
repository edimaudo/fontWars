About fontWars

Can you spot the differences between Arial and Helvetica? Times New Roman and Baskerville? Prove it.

WARNING: FontWars is insanely addictive. Please use responsibly.

  • Easily view your global ranking on the leaderboard
  • Slick 3D spatial and web-responsive interface
  • Unlock new fonts and character weights as you reach higher levels
